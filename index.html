A memory worth keeping

That Evening.

03 · 10 · 2026

Meri life ki pehli date — Kamal Pokhari, ek thandi si shaam, aur meri mohtarma.

It started around 4:30.

Jagah mohtarma ne decide ki hui thi. Mujhe bas pahuchna tha... aur shayad khud ko thoda sambhalna bhi.

Karib 4:30 baje shaam ka waqt tha jab main wahan pahucha. Jaise hi wahan pahucha, sabse pehle maine apna scooter park kiya aur phir main bhagta chala ek chocolate ki talaash mein. Unko Dairy Milk pasand thi aur Dairy Milk kahin mil hi nahi rahi thi. Kareeb 10 minute idhar-udhar dhundne ke baad finally mujhe ek dukaan dikhi jahan woh chocolate available thi.

Kamal Pokhari · That evening

Chocolate li aur phir sab kuch karke hum pahunche Kamal Pokhari. Wahan ka mahaul ekdum saaf, sundar aur logon se bhara hua tha. Ek behtareen shaam thi aur upar se halki si thand — woh proper winter wali feeling.

Jaise-jaise waqt beet raha tha, waise-waise mujhe aur ghabrahat hone lagi ki kab waqt aage badhega, kya baat hogi, kaise milunga usse, kya bolunga... dimaag mein bas yahi sab chal raha tha. Phir maine khud ko samjhaya ki zyada sochenge toh kaam aur bigdega, isliye jo hoga dekha jayega.

Around 5:12 — 5:17 PM
“Kahan ho aap?”

Aur phir meri mohtarma aayi. Mujhe ek call aaya — “Kahan ho aap?” Bas phir meri aankhein unko dekhte hue bheed mein idhar-udhar ho rahi thi. Jab mujhe asli jagah ka pata chala toh main kaise-taise, ghabrate hue unki taraf chal pada.

Maine unko dhund liya tha, lekin aankhon mein ek alag hi sharam thi — “pyaar ki sharam” — isliye nazrein milane se bach raha tha. Woh ek mast white & black duo-colour jacket mein aayi thi aur bahot sundar lag rahi thi.

Aur main? Main toh chappal mein chala gaya tha, like literally... 😂 Lekin jaise hi maine unko saamne dekha, meri saari ghabrahat pata nahi kaise ekdum door ho gayi.

Bas afsos iss baat ka raha ki main unke liye zyada kuch nahi kar paya, aur upar se waqt bhi ekdum limited tha, isliye sab kuch apne hisaab se manage karna possible nahi tha. Lekin phir bhi, jo waqt mila woh mere liye kaafi tha.

And somehow,
it was perfect.

Meri life ki pehli date mere liye ekdum yaadgaar rahi. Personally mujhe bahot maza aaya. Itne waqt se jis din ka intezaar tha, aakhirkaar woh din 3 October 2026 ko aa hi gaya.

Place meri mohtarma ne choose ki thi — aur kya location thi... ekdum maza aa gaya.

Ek shaam, ek mohtarma, thodi si ghabrahat, bahot saari khushi... aur ek yaad.
03 October 2026 · Kamal Pokhari
